# Flag Zombie flag asset

Flag mesh, skin weights and embedded diffuse/normal textures extracted from
“Flag Zombie - PVZ Garden Warfare” by Nazar Okruzhko (Назар Окружко).

Author: https://sketchfab.com/Hot-Dog_1947
Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/flag-zombie-pvz-garden-warfare-c7850fd41b1c4b9b90bf639676f19ac6
License recorded in supplied GLB: CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: flag attached to the project's existing ordinary zombie skeleton with
its original inverse bind matrices; original textures retained; legacy material
converted to metallic/roughness; horizontal holding pose and cloth motion added.
The ordinary zombie body is the previously supplied PvZ_Zombie2.glb asset.
