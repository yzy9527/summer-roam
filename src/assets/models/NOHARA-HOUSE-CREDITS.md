# Crayon Shin-chan Nohara house

Author: sadraccoon03 — https://sketchfab.com/sadraccoon03
Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/crayon-shin-chan-nohara-house-de43eec1ddcf43b29effda8d63d162f4

Runtime adaptation converts legacy specular/glossiness materials to metallic/roughness materials, preserving diffuse colours, embedded images, geometry and transparency. Glossiness is approximated as inverse roughness. Scene placement centres the model, rests it on a level foundation, and rotates its entrance toward the terminal turnaround.
