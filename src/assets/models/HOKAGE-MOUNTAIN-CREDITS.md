# Hokage mountain

Modified for this project: normalized coordinates and scale, removed tiny planar debris, combined meshes by material, conservatively simplified large rock meshes, and added closed sides, back slopes, a level summit, a rock apron and a walkable trail. Original sculpted face and embedded textures retained. User-provided original is preserved at assets-source/mountain/six_hokage_mountain.glb.
